Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Ask on YouTube ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains that our Chrome extension offers optional usage analytics as described below. Usage analytics are off by default and require your explicit choice in Settings.

2. Information We Collect

If you enable usage analytics, the extension sends confirmed question submission events, submission/session timestamps, foreground time spent waiting for submission confirmation, its version and a randomly generated browser-profile identifier directly to Google Analytics. We do not include questions, answers, video identifiers, titles, URLs, transcripts or comments in these analytics events. Google processes your IP address when receiving the connection.

Information used to answer your questions

When you ask a question, the extension processes your question and selected video information, such as the title, URL, captions and comments you choose to include, and sends that content to your chosen AI service. Questions, video information and answer links may be kept in the extension's local question history. These contents are not included in Google Analytics events. If you configure a Gemini API key, it is stored in Chrome sync storage and sent to Google's Gemini service to authenticate the requests you initiate; it is not included in analytics. The AI service's own privacy terms also apply.

3. Use of Information

Optional analytics help us count submissions and understand repeat use. Disabling analytics stops subsequent reports and removes the local analytics identifier. It does not recall in-flight requests or delete events already sent. Enabling it again generates a new identifier.

Google Analytics event-level and user-level retention is configured to two months, without resetting retention on new activity. Google applies retention-setting changes within 24 hours; aggregated reports may be retained longer. Local question history can be managed in the extension's History page. Local usage counters keep a rolling 90-day window when updated.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

When you opt in, usage analytics are sent directly to Google Analytics; no separate collection server is used. Advertising user data and ad personalization are denied in these requests. Contact us below about privacy questions and deletion requests. We do not sell user data or use it for advertising, creditworthiness or lending. Our use of data adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google's handling is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security

The optional on-device usage statistics setting is off by default. When enabled, it stores daily counts of confirmed ChatGPT question submissions locally in your browser, using UTC dates. It does not store question text, answers, video details, or user identifiers and does not transmit these statistics to us or third parties. Old daily entries are removed when statistics are updated. Turning the setting off deletes the statistics. You can explicitly export them to a local JSON file; exporting does not upload or share the file.

Analytics requests use HTTPS. Browser-profile identifiers are stored locally, not synchronized to other devices. Analytics failures do not prevent asking questions. This notice describes the optional analytics; question content still goes to the AI service you use to obtain answers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
